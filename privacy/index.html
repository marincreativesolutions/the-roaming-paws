Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Effective date: June 19, 2026  ·  Marin Creative Solutions LLC

1. Who We Are

The Roaming Paws™ is a brand of Marin Creative Solutions LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit theroamingpaws.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Contact form: When you contact us via our contact form (powered by Formspree), we receive your name, email address, and message.
  • Newsletter: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address via MailerLite.
  • Payments: When you make a purchase, payment information is collected and processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your credit card details.
  • Usage data: We may collect basic, anonymized information about how you use our website (page visits, browser type) to improve our services.

3. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your cookie consent preference (stored locally on your device).
  • Enable secure payment processing (Stripe sets cookies during checkout for fraud prevention).
  • Load fonts from Google Fonts (this may send your IP address to Google’s servers).

You can dismiss our cookie banner at any time. Refusing cookies may limit some functionality, such as payment processing.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and messages.
  • To send newsletters and updates you have opted into.
  • To process and fulfill your orders.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Formspree — contact form processing (formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy)
  • MailerLite — email newsletters (mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Google Fonts — typography (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Prodigi — print-on-demand fulfillment (prodigi.com/privacy-policy)

6. Data Retention

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time. Order data is retained as required by law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, please contact us.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website.
