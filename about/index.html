Our Story

About The Roaming Paws™

Born at the intersection of photography, technology, family, and one very adventurous dog.

The Roaming Paws™ was born at the intersection of all the things we love most — photography, technology, family, and a small dog named Luna who changed everything.

Before Luna arrived, Oliver had already been everywhere. By car, by plane, across mountains and cities and places most people only dream of visiting. A loyal companion on every journey, quietly taking it all in.

Then Luna came along — and the adventures got a lot louder.

Every place in these stories is real. Every photograph was taken with care. And every story was written with the hope that somewhere, a small reader decides to be just a little bit braver today.

These stories are about discovering the world — and discovering yourself along the way.

— The Roaming Paws™ Creator

Meet the Adventurers

Luna The Trailblazer

A playful, curious Shih Tzu with a personality ten times her size. Luna loves playing, keeping her humans company at all times, and being just stubborn enough to get exactly what she wants — which is almost always a treat. She is the voice of curiosity, charm, and quiet bravery in every story we tell.

Oliver The Guide

The seasoned explorer who paved the way. Wise, calm, and incredibly loyal, Oliver traveled thousands of miles across mountains, lakes, and forests. He has walked mountain trails, crossed state lines, and stood at the edge of places most people only dream of seeing. Every destination in these stories carries a piece of him.

Our Philosophy

🏔️

Real Locations

Every mountain peak, lake shore, and forest path shown in our books is a real place. We hike these trails ourselves to capture their true magic, bridging the gap between child readers and the great outdoors.

📸

Real Photography

Instead of fully illustrated landscapes, our books feature real-world photography integrated with custom illustrations. This unique style inspires kids to look at the real world with a sense of wonder and curiosity.

💛

Emotional Growth

Luna's adventures teach little ones that it's okay to feel nervous or afraid. True bravery isn't about having no fear; it's about taking that next step on the trail even when you do.
